\label{sec:ablations}

The ablations compare three conditions with common sample IDs and budgets: \emph{basic tools}
(the interface of Section~\ref{sec:exp-tools}), \emph{legal enumeration} (adding
\texttt{list\_legal\_folds}), and \emph{automatic feedback} (adding tier-3 target feedback after
every edit). Earlier runs instead let the model request tier-3 comparison on demand.

\label{sec:target-feedback}
The automatic-feedback condition supplies layer counts, aligned layer discrepancies,
and suggested corrections after edits (Appendix~\ref{app:feedback-levels}). Because those
corrections do much of the target comparison for the model, we treat this condition as an upper
bound on what tool support can achieve, not as a measure of the model's own geometric reasoning;
conclusions about the model rest on the basic-tools condition. Even that condition is assisted,
since folds that would crease outside the target pattern are rejected (Section~\ref{sec:env}).
