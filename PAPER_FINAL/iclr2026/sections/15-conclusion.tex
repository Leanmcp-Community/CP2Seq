\section{Conclusion and future work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
CP2Seq supplies generated, reachable targets and executable sequence planning with
identity-aware verification. Matched search baselines show that model failures on
easy, some-layers and medium instances do not imply unreachable targets. Images add
no measured benefit over exact numeric state; assistance and effort improve some
short-sequence totals. Repeated trials, stopping audits and trajectory analysis
characterize observable behavior while exposing variance, comparison-tool defects
and depth--layer confounding. Future work should evaluate the layer-capped corpus,
repeat every condition, expand model coverage and test controlled support
interventions before attributing failure to internal capabilities.
