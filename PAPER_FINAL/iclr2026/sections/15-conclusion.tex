\section{Conclusion and future work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

CP2Seq evaluates geometric planning through executable folding sequences. It provides
600 procedural instances, an interactive folding environment, and evaluation of both
crease patterns and target states. The Luna ablations show improved some-layers
performance with higher reasoning effort, while medium and hard samples remain
unsolved. BFS provides a separate measure of performance under systematic search.
Future experiments will examine state tracking and backtracking, expand model and
sample coverage, and compare model and search performance under matched budgets.
