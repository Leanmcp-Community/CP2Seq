\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\paragraph{Scope and difficulty.}
The environment models zero-thickness flat states and simple folds, without material
mechanics or continuous collision checks. It is not a physical folding simulator.
Reference depth need not be minimum solution depth. In the uncapped all-layers release,
depth and layer count are confounded (medium and hard medians 94 and 404 layers;
maximum 16,384), so hard-group failure is not an isolated planning-depth limit.
A layer-capped corpus was generated, but no reported model result uses it.

\paragraph{Evaluation and uncertainty.}
Most ablations use one episode per instance and ten instances per group. The repeated
Luna trials show substantial variance; five-trial coverage uses five times the budget.
Sonnet extends family coverage but does not support broad rankings. Protocol failures
count as unsolved. The original comparison tool used $E_{\mathrm{plane}}$ and could
falsely report a match; identity-aware re-scoring corrects terminal outcomes, not the
feedback already shown to models (Appendices~\ref{app:identity-audit}
and~\ref{app:protocol-failures}).
For the separate bottom-layer alignment defect, a call-level audit of 65 saved
non-ablation episodes finds one divergent call among 1,025 comparisons; two additional
calls report a plane-only match that fails identity-aware scoring. Missing image-run
logs prevent a complete historical audit. Affected cases have not been rerun, and
the direction of the feedback effect remains unknown.
An offline stricter cycling threshold can truncate saved trajectories, but a relaxed
threshold requires new continuations. We have not run those continuations and do not
claim invariance to the stopping rule (Appendix~\ref{app:matched-trajectories}).

\paragraph{Interpretation and reproducibility.}
Revisits, restarts and stopping labels locate behavior without identifying an internal
memory or reasoning deficit. Planned support interventions have not been evaluated.
Per-turn logs survive only for the text-only runs; image-run feedback cannot be audited
call by call. Cycling limits and unequal episode lengths affect observed exploration.
Corpus replay shares the generation engine, numerical checks use stated tolerances,
closed model weights cannot be pinned, and training overlap is unknown. Extended
limitations and repeated-trial details appear in Appendix~\ref{app:extended-limitations}.
