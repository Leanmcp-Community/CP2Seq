\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The current evaluation is concentrated on Luna and the search baseline; other model runs have
limited coverage. All 461 model attempts with a recorded reasoning setting used low effort, while
25 lack that setting. The aggregate contains repeated attempts and does not establish matched
samples and budgets across configurations. Higher-effort runs and controlled assistance comparisons
remain to be completed.

Fold-count groups express the hypothesis that longer reference sequences generally require more
search. They do not establish minimum solution length or guarantee an ordering of instance
difficulty. The action space covers all-layers and selected some-layers simple folds, excluding
pre-creasing; the some-layers corpus currently extends to six reference folds. The environment
represents zero-thickness flat states and does not model material mechanics or continuous
collisions during folding.

Success requires geometric reasoning, state tracking, and planning together. Termination labels
identify observed behavior but do not isolate the underlying cause of failure. Revisit and budget
limits also constrain the search trajectories that can be observed. Generating new samples reduces
reliance on a fixed public test set but cannot guarantee absence of training overlap.

Corpus replay checks share the generation engine and therefore test consistency rather than
independent physical validity. Final comparisons use numerical tolerances. The terminal comparator
compares current-plane polygons, parity and rank, and does not explicitly match original-sheet
region identities, as detailed in Appendix~\ref{app:comparison-details}; equal shapes at different
original-sheet locations can therefore require a correspondence check this path does not perform.
