\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Every model attempt ran at low reasoning effort.} All 461 language-model attempts with a
recorded setting used \texttt{reasoning\_effort: low}. The results support a claim about models at
that setting, not about frontier models in general, and the headline is worded accordingly. Raising
the effort on the best-covered arm is the most valuable remaining experiment.

\textbf{The easy stratum does not measure what the benchmark is for.} Deterministic breadth-first
search solves 54.7\% of it at a median of 65 states expanded, beating every model. The stratum
measures whether a model can beat trivial search on a tree its own tool has pruned roughly
570-fold. Claims about reasoning should be read off mid and hard, where every arm including search
is at or near zero, and where the benchmark currently discriminates nothing either. \textbf{The band
in which this benchmark separates models may be narrow or, at this depth range, empty.}

\textbf{Model coverage is severely imbalanced.} 447 attempts for one model and between one and seven
for four others. No cross-model comparison is supported.

\textbf{No training.} Every arm is an off-the-shelf model driven by prompting and tool calling; no
model is trained or fine-tuned. The contribution is the harness, not a model. This is stated in the
introduction as well as here, because a limitation the authors declare is context and one a
reviewer discovers is an objection.

\textbf{Scope of the action space.} 89.3\%% fact:probeC.provenNotPct
of the 366% fact:corpus.instagram.total
real crease patterns in Flat-Folder's \texttt{examples/instagram/} corpus lie outside all-layers
simple folding, and pre-creasing, the operation real
folders use to reach long sequences at low coupling, is excluded.

\textbf{The corpus verifiers share an engine with the generator}, so they cannot detect a wrong
model of paper. They detect drift between what was folded and what was recorded.

\textbf{Attributing failures to individual capabilities.} Successful construction requires
geometric reasoning, spatial state tracking, and sequential planning together. A failed attempt
alone does not identify which capability failed. Isolating their contributions requires targeted
experiments; changing tool availability does not necessarily isolate a single capability.

\textbf{Generation parameters and difficulty.} Reference sequence depth is not a guarantee of
minimum solution length, and variation across generated instances does not by itself establish greater
difficulty. Difficulty must also be assessed through solver performance.

\textbf{Evaluation contamination.} Generating fresh instances reduces reliance on a fixed public
test set, but does not guarantee freedom from training overlap. Models may have encountered the
generator, related folding procedures, or structurally similar examples.

\textbf{Flat states only.} The environment represents flat-folded states with explicit layer
ordering. It excludes paper thickness, material mechanics, and continuous collision checking
during folding motions. Passing the engine's checks therefore establishes validity within the
supported discrete model, not full physical realizability.

\textbf{Level 2's group is narrower than the truest notion of equality}, because valid layer-order
variants are excluded for the reason given in Section~\ref{sec:scoring-what}. It is therefore
strict rather than permissive, which is the safe direction but not a free one.
