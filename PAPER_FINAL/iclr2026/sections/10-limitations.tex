\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The evaluation concentrates on Luna and BFS, with limited coverage of other models.
The recent low- and high-effort ablations use ten samples per group and configuration.
Both limits are set by cost rather than by the benchmark: with full image history, one episode
consumed about 3.1 million input tokens on average over 20 measured episodes, so every
additional model or configuration on the full corpus is a substantial expense for an unfunded
study.

The action space covers all-layers and selected some-layers simple folds, excluding
pre-creasing; the some-layers corpus currently extends to six reference folds. The environment
represents zero-thickness flat states and does not model material mechanics or continuous
collisions during folding.

Termination labels record stopping conditions; attributing failures to individual capabilities
requires further trajectory analysis. Revisit and budget limits constrain the observed search.
Training overlap with the public corpus is unknown.

Corpus replay checks assess consistency with the shared generation engine. The terminal comparator
compares current-plane polygons, parity and rank, and does not explicitly match original-sheet
region identities, as detailed in Appendix~\ref{app:comparison-details}; equal shapes at different
original-sheet locations can therefore require a correspondence check this path does not perform.
