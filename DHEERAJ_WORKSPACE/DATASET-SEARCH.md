# Dataset search — who actually has CP → *sequence*?

2026-09-15. Searched for datasets giving **step-by-step folding sequences**, not just endpoints.

## Answer

**Yes — PurelandFold is the only real one, and it's 27 sequences.** Everything else either stops at
the crease pattern, stops at the final state, generates sequences rather than shipping them, or was
never released.

| Source | CP | Sequence | Usable? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PurelandFold** | ✅ | ✅ **27** | ✅ the only real one — too small for a headline |
| Creasy | ✅ | ✅ generated on demand | ⚠️ generator, few models, expensive |
| Learn2Fold / OrigamiCode | ✅ | ✅ 5,760 | ❌ **never released** |
| Flat-Folder `instagram/` (= OrigamiBench) | ✅ 366 | ❌ | CP supply only |
| GamiBench | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ images + labels, not geometry |
| OrigamiSpace | ? | ? | ❌ no repo |
| edemaine/fold, Origami Simulator | ✅ some | ❌ mostly | unaudited, low priority |

**New finding this search:** PurelandFold is not standalone — it is **FoldingAgent's** dataset
(arXiv 2609.00377). Our notes list the two separately, PurelandFold as data and FoldingAgent as a
cite-only method. Same project. Worth fixing.

## Per source: what's there, what isn't

### 1. PurelandFold — the only real CP → Seq source
- **Link**: huggingface.co/datasets/mayaweiz/PurelandFold · **License**: CC-BY-4.0
- **Has**: 27 folding sequences / 337 frames. Keyframe-level ground truth per step — folding action,
  paper topology, vertex + face geometry, crease assignments, face orientations, **layer structure**
  (`cp.fold`). Derived from OrigamiWay "Easy Origami" (60 models, 40 Pureland).
- **Doesn't have**: scale (27), difficulty spread — non-local-dependency p50 = 0.7 vs instagram's
  12.8 (**~20× narrower**, almost no spread internally), so `step` (5–21) is the only usable axis.
  Not representative of compound folds.
- **Open**: frame → per-step `.fold` conversion unconfirmed; **74 rows skipped (`variables=0`)** —
  27 are step-1, ~47 unexplained.
- **Origin caveat**: video-derived (annotated from demonstration videos), not symbolic ground truth.

### 2. Creasy / Akitaya 2013 — a generator, not a dataset
- **Link**: github.com/xkevio/Creasy · **License**: GPL-3
- **Has**: computes a full step-graph for any CP its **4 maneuvers** handle (inside/outside reverse
  fold, swivel ×2). For crane / traditional frog base that's genuine step-by-step ground truth,
  sometimes tens of thousands of nodes deep.
- **Doesn't have**: coverage (most real CPs fall outside 4 maneuvers), new CPs, cheapness (frog base:
  ~30 min, 22,665 nodes). Unmaintained since 2022, Java+JavaFX + hand-installed ORIPA.
- **Use**: a handful of high-quality reference sequences + the published baseline numbers.

### 3. Learn2Fold / OrigamiCode — has exactly what we want, can't have it
- **Link**: arXiv 2603.29585 · **Data: NOT RELEASED**
- **Would have had**: 5,760 sequences / 75,000 verified transitions, 25 object classes, 3 difficulty
  tiers, per-step mesh geometry + crease states + fold parameters. Mostly generated by their own
  symbolic simulator.
- **Doesn't have**: a download. Nothing to audit or convert.
- **Takeaway**: their corpus being ~synthetic is the precedent for ours being synthetic.

### 4. Flat-Folder `examples/instagram/` (= OrigamiBench's dataset)
- **Link**: github.com/origamimagiro/flat-folder · **License**: MIT
- **Has**: 366 `.fold` crease patterns. A solver that computes *a* terminal state on demand.
- **Doesn't have**: any sequence. No stored terminal label either — and a CP has many valid states,
  so picking one is our experimental choice.
- ⚠️ **125 / 366 = 34.2% are provably NOT simple-foldable** → no solution in our action space at all.

### 5. GamiBench — audited this session, **closes an open action item**
- **Link**: huggingface.co/datasets/stvngo/GamiBench · **License**: MIT
- **Has**: 777 rows, 45.5 MB, `imagefolder` format — fields are just `image` + `label`, 186 label
  classes (e.g. `04x4_checkerboard`, `15x5_grid`).
- **Doesn't have**: sequences, `.fold` geometry, CP → Seq pairs of any kind. It's a
  visual QA / classification benchmark.
- **Verdict**: **not a data source.** Metric definitions only (Accuracy, Viewpoint Consistency,
  Impossible Fold Selection Rate). Action item can be closed.

### 6. OrigamiSpace — still inaccessible
- **Link**: arXiv 2511.18450 (NeurIPS'25) · **no public repo**
- Fetched the paper: it's an MLLM spatial-reasoning benchmark; dataset composition, format and
  release are not specified in the text. Cites origami-database.com, flat-folder, ORIPA as sources.
- **Verdict**: unchanged — recheck periodically.

### 7. Generic FOLD sources — unaudited, lowest priority
- github.com/edemaine/fold · origamisimulator.org
- **Note worth having**: the FOLD spec *does* support sequences — `frame_classes: "diagrams"` is
  explicitly "a sequence of frames representing folding steps." **The format supports what we need;
  almost nobody populates it.** That's the gap in one line.

## What this means

1. **The synthetic corpus isn't a fallback, it's the only option.** Confirmed by search, not assumed:
   27 real sequences (PurelandFold) + a handful from a 2013 generator (Creasy) is the entire world
   supply of CP → Seq ground truth. The one corpus with scale was never released.
2. **PurelandFold is the reality anchor** — it validates the loop and calibrates the synthetic
   corpus's `step` axis. It cannot carry a headline.
3. **Two notes to fix**: merge PurelandFold and FoldingAgent (same project); close the GamiBench
   audit item as "no sequences, metrics only."
